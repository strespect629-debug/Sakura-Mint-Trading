Sakura Mint Trading

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sakura Mint Trading collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information in connection with its sourcing, purchasing, and export support services.

By submitting a request, approving a quotation, authorizing a purchase, making a payment, or communicating with Sakura Mint Trading, the buyer agrees to the handling of personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Sakura Mint Trading may collect the following information from buyers:

  • Full name;
  • Company name, if applicable;
  • Country;
  • Delivery address;
  • Email address;
  • WhatsApp number or other contact information;
  • Product or vehicle request details;
  • Reference URLs, quotations, or supporting documents provided by the buyer;
  • Quotation approval, purchase authorization, payment confirmation, and transaction records;
  • Shipping, customs, and delivery information;
  • Communications between the buyer and Sakura Mint Trading.

2. How We Use Information

Sakura Mint Trading uses collected information for the following purposes:

  • To review sourcing or export support requests;
  • To prepare quotations;
  • To communicate with the buyer;
  • To confirm quotation approval and purchase authorization;
  • To process payments and confirm payment status;
  • To purchase, source, inspect, arrange, or ship products or vehicles;
  • To prepare shipping, customs, export, and delivery-related documents;
  • To manage transaction records;
  • To prevent fraud, prohibited transactions, sanctions violations, illegal activity, or compliance risks;
  • To respond to questions, claims, disputes, or legal requests;
  • To improve Sakura Mint Trading’s services.

3. Sharing of Information

Sakura Mint Trading may share necessary information with third parties only when required for transaction, payment, sourcing, export, shipping, customs, compliance, legal, or business purposes.

Such third parties may include:

  • Suppliers;
  • Dealers;
  • Auction houses;
  • Inspection providers;
  • Shipping carriers;
  • Freight forwarders;
  • Customs brokers;
  • Warehouses;
  • Ports;
  • Payment-related financial institutions;
  • Professional advisors;
  • Government authorities or regulatory bodies where required by law.

Sakura Mint Trading does not sell buyers’ personal information.

4. International Transactions

Because Sakura Mint Trading provides sourcing and export support services from Japan to buyers in other countries, personal information may be used, transferred, or processed across borders when necessary for communication, sourcing, payment, shipping, customs, export, delivery, or legal compliance.

5. Data Storage and Security

Sakura Mint Trading stores personal information only to the extent necessary for service operation, transaction management, accounting, legal compliance, dispute handling, and business recordkeeping.

Sakura Mint Trading takes reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and Sakura Mint Trading cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention of Information

Sakura Mint Trading may retain personal information and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary for transaction management, accounting, tax, legal, compliance, dispute resolution, and business recordkeeping purposes.

7. Buyer Responsibilities

The buyer is responsible for providing accurate, complete, and up-to-date information.

If the buyer provides information about another person or company, the buyer is responsible for ensuring that they have the right or permission to provide such information to Sakura Mint Trading.

8. Requests Regarding Personal Information

Buyers may contact Sakura Mint Trading to request confirmation, correction, or deletion of their personal information, where applicable.

Sakura Mint Trading may refuse or limit such requests where retention is necessary for legal, accounting, tax, compliance, transaction, dispute, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

9. Cookies and Website Analytics

The Sakura Mint Trading website may use basic website functions or third-party services that collect technical information such as browser type, device information, access time, and usage data.

If Sakura Mint Trading introduces cookies, analytics tools, advertising tools, or tracking technologies in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sakura Mint Trading may update this Privacy Policy when necessary.

The updated version will be posted on the Sakura Mint Trading website with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the “Questions or Additional Information” field in the Japan Sourcing Request Form available on the Sakura Mint Trading website.
