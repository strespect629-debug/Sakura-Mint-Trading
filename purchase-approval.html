Sakura Mint Trading

Quotation Approval & Purchase Authorization

Effective Date: July 5, 2026

This page explains the meaning of approving a quotation and authorizing Sakura Mint Trading to proceed with purchase or sourcing activities.

By submitting the Quotation Approval & Purchase Authorization Form, the buyer confirms that they have reviewed the quotation and agree to the terms described on this page.

1. Quotation Approval

The buyer confirms that they have reviewed the quotation issued by Sakura Mint Trading.

The quotation may include the product or vehicle details, quotation number, quotation currency, total estimated amount, service fees, estimated shipping costs, payment terms, validity period, and important notes.

By approving the quotation, the buyer agrees to the quoted amount, currency, scope of service, and transaction conditions stated in the quotation.

2. Purchase Authorization

By submitting the Quotation Approval & Purchase Authorization Form, the buyer authorizes Sakura Mint Trading to proceed according to the approved quotation.

For new Japanese products, Sakura Mint Trading will begin purchasing only after the required quotation approval and payment confirmation.

For used vehicles, vehicle search, review of available vehicle options, and quotation preparation may take place before purchase authorization. Sakura Mint Trading will not purchase, reserve, bid on, or otherwise commit to acquiring a vehicle until the required quotation approval, payment confirmation, and any required final purchase authorization have been completed.

For certain transactions, such as used vehicles, auction items, substitute products, or items requiring condition confirmation, final purchase authorization may also be required before purchase.

3. Payment Confirmation

Sakura Mint Trading will not purchase new products or purchase, reserve, bid on, or otherwise commit to acquiring a used vehicle until receipt of the required payment has been confirmed.

The buyer understands that Sakura Mint Trading does not purchase, reserve, or secure products or vehicles before payment confirmation unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

Bank transfer fees, intermediary bank fees, currency conversion fees, and other payment-related charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless otherwise stated in the quotation.

If the received amount is less than the quoted total amount, the buyer may be asked to pay the difference before a new product purchase or a vehicle purchase, reservation, bid, or other acquisition commitment is made.

4. Price, Availability, and Cost Changes

The buyer understands that product availability, vehicle availability, supplier prices, exchange rates, shipping costs, taxes, fees, and other related costs may change before a product purchase or vehicle acquisition commitment is made.

If a significant change occurs before a product purchase or vehicle acquisition commitment is made, Sakura Mint Trading will notify the buyer and may revise or cancel the quotation as necessary.

Approval of a quotation does not guarantee that the requested product or vehicle will remain available until payment confirmation and purchase completion.

5. Cancellation, Changes, and Refunds

The buyer understands that cancellation, changes, or refunds may not be possible after an authorized product purchase or a vehicle purchase, reservation, bid, or other acquisition commitment has been made or begun.

If cancellation or refund is possible, supplier charges, bank fees, intermediary bank fees, currency conversion losses, handling costs, shipping costs, storage costs, or other expenses already incurred may be deducted from the refund.

Refund timing and method may depend on the payment method, financial institutions, supplier policies, shipping status, and transaction circumstances.

6. Import Duties, Taxes, and Destination Charges

The buyer understands that import duties, taxes, customs fees, inspection fees, registration fees, permits, certifications, storage charges, and other destination-country charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless clearly included in the quotation.

The buyer is responsible for confirming whether the requested product or vehicle can be legally imported, registered, used, or resold in the destination country.

Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee customs clearance, import approval, registration, certification, or legal use in the buyer’s country.

7. Product and Vehicle Condition

New products will be sourced according to the product details stated in the quotation or purchase authorization.

Sakura Mint Trading currently accepts used vehicles only. Other used consumer goods are not supported unless Sakura Mint Trading expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

Used vehicles are supplied in their existing condition at the time of purchase.

The buyer is responsible for reviewing all available product information, vehicle information, photographs, inspection information, auction sheets, supplier descriptions, and related documents before giving final purchase authorization.

10. Digital Signature

By entering their full name in the Quotation Approval & Purchase Authorization Form, the buyer confirms that the entered name acts as their digital signature for the approval and authorization.

The buyer agrees that the form submission, quotation number, email address, date, digital signature, and related electronic records may be used as evidence of the buyer’s approval and instructions.

11. Contact

Questions regarding quotation approval or purchase authorization may be submitted through the “Questions or Additional Information” field in the Japan Sourcing Request Form available on the Sakura Mint Trading website.
