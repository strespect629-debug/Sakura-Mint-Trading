Used Vehicle Sourcing from Japan

Sakura Mint Trading helps overseas buyers request quotations for used vehicles from Japan and supports the sourcing and export-related process after approval and payment confirmation.

What We Support

Used Vehicle Sourcing Support

We assist overseas buyers with the following parts of the used vehicle sourcing process:

  • Used vehicle sourcing requests from overseas buyers
  • Quotation preparation
  • Vehicle information review support
  • Supplier, dealer, or auction-related communication support
  • Purchase support after quotation approval and payment confirmation
  • Export-related support from Japan
Important Notes for Used Vehicles

Important Considerations

Used vehicles are handled with special care and attention because the buyer must make an informed decision before purchase.

  • Sakura Mint Trading currently accepts used vehicles as supported used items.
  • Used vehicles are supplied in their existing condition at the time of purchase.
  • Vehicle condition, mileage, repair history, auction sheet information, photos, and supplier descriptions may not reveal every issue.
  • The buyer is responsible for reviewing all available information before final purchase authorization.
  • Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee registration, customs clearance, certification, legal use, or resale in the buyer’s country.
  • Import duties, taxes, customs fees, registration fees, inspection fees, and destination-country charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless clearly stated in the quotation.
How the Process Works

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Submit a sourcing request

Provide your request for a used vehicle sourcing inquiry through the request form.

Step 2: Sakura Mint Trading reviews the request

We review the request and assess the available sourcing options.

Step 3: A quotation is prepared

We prepare the quotation based on the requested vehicle and related support requirements.

Step 4: The buyer reviews and approves the quotation

Quotation approval is required before any purchase or sourcing activity proceeds.

Step 5: Payment instructions are provided

Payment instructions are shared after quotation review and approval.

Step 6: Purchase or sourcing activities begin after payment confirmation

All purchase or sourcing activities begin only after payment confirmation.

Step 7: Export-related and shipping support proceeds as applicable

Export-related assistance and shipping coordination proceed based on the agreed scope.
