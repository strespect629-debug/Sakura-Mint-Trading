Time-Sensitive Bidding

Auction Purchases

Some vehicles, particularly rare or limited-availability vehicles, may be offered through time-sensitive auctions. International payments may not arrive before the auction deadline.

Buyers who wish to participate in vehicle auctions may therefore be required to deposit sufficient bidding funds with Sakura Mint Trading in advance. Sakura Mint Trading will only place bids within the maximum bid amount authorized by the buyer and subject to confirmed available funds.

Depositing bidding funds does not guarantee that a vehicle will be successfully purchased. If a bid is unsuccessful, the remaining funds may be retained for a future purchase authorized by the buyer or refunded upon request, subject to applicable bank fees, intermediary bank fees, currency conversion differences, and any other costs disclosed in advance.

Auction availability, bidding eligibility, bidding deadlines, vehicle condition, auction rules, and other transaction conditions may vary. Sakura Mint Trading may decline or be unable to place a bid where sufficient funds, buyer authorization, required information, or sufficient time to complete the necessary procedures are not available.

Clear Cost Review

What the Quotation May Include

Depending on the transaction and the information available, the quotation may show:

  • Vehicle price
  • Sakura Mint Trading service or support charges
  • Estimated Japan-side costs
  • Estimated shipping costs, when available
  • Other applicable charges

Estimated amounts may change if carrier rates, schedules, port charges, vehicle details, or other circumstances change. The quotation does not guarantee destination-country duties, taxes, registration, inspection, compliance, or other import-side costs.

Review Before Approval

Vehicle Information

Before making a purchase decision, buyers can review the vehicle information that is reasonably available for the proposed vehicle. Depending on the source and vehicle, this may include:

  • Photos
  • Mileage
  • Vehicle specifications
  • Condition information
  • Auction sheet or inspection information, when available
  • Supplier information

Information availability and detail vary by supplier and vehicle. Buyers should review all available material carefully before authorizing a purchase.

Important Notes for Used Vehicles

Important Considerations

Used vehicles are handled with special care and attention because the buyer must make an informed decision before purchase.

  • Sakura Mint Trading currently accepts used vehicles as supported used items.
  • Used vehicles are supplied in their existing condition at the time of purchase.
  • Vehicle condition, mileage, repair history, auction sheet information, photos, and supplier descriptions may not reveal every issue.
  • The buyer is responsible for reviewing all available information before final purchase authorization.
  • Most vehicles sourced from Japan are right-hand drive (RHD).
  • Buyers are responsible for confirming whether right-hand drive vehicles can be legally imported, registered, used, or resold in their destination country.
  • Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee registration, customs clearance, certification, legal use, or resale in the buyer’s country.
  • Import duties, taxes, customs fees, registration fees, inspection fees, and destination-country charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless clearly stated in the quotation.
How the Process Works

Request, Approval, Purchase and Export

Step 1: Request

Submit a used vehicle sourcing inquiry through the request form and tell us what you need.

Step 2: Vehicle search

We review the request and assess available sourcing options in Japan.

Step 3: Quotation

We prepare a quotation based on the requested vehicle and related support requirements.

Step 4: Buyer review

Review the quotation and all vehicle information made available before deciding whether to proceed.

Step 5: Approval

Your quotation approval is required before we purchase, reserve, bid on, or otherwise commit to acquiring a vehicle on your behalf.

Step 6: Payment confirmation

Payment instructions are shared after approval. We confirm the required payment before committing to the purchase or acquisition of a vehicle.

Step 7: Final purchase authorization, if required

For transactions requiring final purchase authorization, we obtain the buyer’s authorization before committing to the vehicle purchase or acquisition.

Step 8: Purchase / acquisition

After the required quotation approval, payment confirmation, and any required final purchase authorization have been completed, we proceed with the authorized vehicle purchase or acquisition steps.

Step 9: Export and shipping coordination

Export-related assistance and shipping coordination proceed as applicable, based on the agreed scope.

However, we will not purchase, reserve, bid on, or otherwise commit to acquiring a vehicle on your behalf until the required quotation approval, payment confirmation, and any required final purchase authorization have been completed. You have the opportunity to review the available vehicle information and quoted transaction terms before authorizing us to proceed.
