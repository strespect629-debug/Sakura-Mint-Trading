Notice

Important Notice

Please Read Before Ordering

  • Buyers are responsible for confirming whether requested products or vehicles can be legally imported into their country.
  • Import and export regulations vary by country. Buyers must confirm import restrictions, customs duties, taxes, permits, manufacturer restrictions, country-specific prohibitions, and shipping limitations before placing an order.
  • Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee that every requested item can be purchased, exported, or imported.
  • No purchase will be made without buyer approval of the quotation.
  • All purchases are made only after payment confirmation.
  • Sakura Mint Trading does not purchase products or vehicles before payment is received.
  • International shipping costs do not include import duties, taxes, customs fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you source used vehicles?

Yes, we can assist with used vehicle sourcing from Japan.

Can you purchase products from Japanese websites?

Yes, we can assist with new Japanese product sourcing from domestic retailers and online platforms.

Do you handle used products?

Currently, used items are limited to vehicles only. We do not handle used consumer goods, used electronics, or used brand items at this time.

When do I pay?

Payment is requested only after you have reviewed and approved the quotation. No payment is required at the inquiry stage.

Can all products be exported?

Not necessarily. Availability depends on export regulations, import rules in your country, supplier conditions, and shipping limitations. We will advise on feasibility during the review stage.

What currencies do you accept?

We accept payments in USD (United States Dollar) and JPY (Japanese Yen). The payment currency will be specified in the quotation.
