About Sakura Mint Trading

Sakura Mint Trading is a Japan-based sourcing and export support service for overseas buyers seeking new Japanese products and used vehicles.

Our Role

We support international buyers by communicating with Japanese suppliers, retailers, manufacturers, and exporters, and by coordinating sourcing and export-related arrangements.

Purchasing and sourcing activities begin only after the buyer approves the quotation and payment has been confirmed.

Used Goods Dealer License (古物商許可): 551320217400

What We Focus On

  • New Japanese product sourcing
  • Used vehicle sourcing from Japan
  • Direct supplier communication
  • Quotation and purchase coordination
  • Export and international shipping support

Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers are responsible for confirming import restrictions, customs duties, taxes, permits, registration requirements, and other destination-country rules before approving a quotation.

Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee that every requested product or vehicle can be purchased, exported, imported, registered, or legally used in the destination country.
