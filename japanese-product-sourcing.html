Japan Sourcing & Export

Japanese Product Sourcing from Japan

Sakura Mint Trading helps overseas buyers request quotations for new Japanese products, review sourcing options, communicate with Japanese suppliers, and coordinate approved purchases and export-related support. Actual product purchases begin only after the required quotation approval and payment confirmation.

What We Support

New Product Sourcing Support

We assist overseas buyers with the following parts of the new product sourcing process:

  • New Japanese product sourcing requests from overseas buyers
  • Product quotation preparation
  • Supplier, retailer, wholesaler, or manufacturer communication support
  • Purchase support after quotation approval and payment confirmation
  • Japan domestic coordination when applicable
  • Export-related support from Japan
Suitable Product Requests

Common Product Request Types

We can support sourcing requests for products that are suitable for quotation review and follow-up purchase support.

  • Japanese consumer products
  • Retail products
  • Wholesale product requests
  • Small business sourcing requests
  • Products with reference URLs, model numbers, photos, or brand information
  • Requests that can be legally purchased, exported, imported, used, or resold
Important Notes

Important Considerations

New product sourcing requires careful review before purchase and shipment.

  • Sakura Mint Trading supports sourcing of new Japanese products.
  • Product availability, supplier prices, shipping costs, and exchange rates may change before payment confirmation.
  • Sourcing review and quotation preparation may occur before purchase. Actual product purchases begin only after the required quotation approval and payment confirmation.
  • The buyer is responsible for confirming whether the requested product can be legally imported, used, or resold in the destination country.
  • Import duties, taxes, customs fees, permits, certifications, and destination-country charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless clearly stated in the quotation.
  • Sakura Mint Trading may refuse requests involving prohibited, restricted, unsafe, counterfeit, illegal, or unsuitable items.
How the Process Works

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Submit a sourcing request

Provide your sourcing request through the request form with product details and reference information.

Step 2: Sakura Mint Trading reviews the product information

We review the information provided and assess sourcing feasibility.

Step 3: A quotation is prepared

We prepare a quotation based on the requested product and related support requirements.

Step 4: The buyer reviews and approves the quotation

Quotation approval is required before Sakura Mint Trading proceeds with the product purchase.

Step 5: Payment instructions are provided

Payment instructions are shared after quotation review and approval.

Step 6: Product purchase begins after payment confirmation

After the required quotation approval and payment confirmation have been completed, Sakura Mint Trading proceeds with the authorized product purchase.

Step 7: Export-related and shipping support proceeds as applicable

Export-related assistance and shipping coordination proceed based on the agreed scope.
