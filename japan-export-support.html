Japan Export Support for Overseas Buyers

Sakura Mint Trading supports overseas buyers with Japan-based sourcing, quotation preparation, supplier communication, purchase support, and export-related coordination after approval and payment confirmation.

What We Support

Japan-Based Export Support Services

We support overseas buyers with the following aspects of Japan-side sourcing and export coordination:

  • Japan-based sourcing support
  • Quotation preparation
  • Supplier, retailer, dealer, or auction-related communication support
  • Purchase support after quotation approval and payment confirmation
  • Japan domestic coordination when applicable
  • Export-related shipping and documentation support when applicable
Supported Requests

Common Request Types

We can support requests that are suitable for review, quotation preparation, and follow-up purchase support.

  • New Japanese products
  • Used vehicles from Japan
  • Product requests with reference URLs, photos, brand names, model numbers, or supplier information
  • Overseas buyer requests requiring Japan-side communication or coordination
  • Requests that can be legally purchased, exported, imported, used, or resold
Important Notes

Important Considerations

Export support and sourcing work requires careful review before purchase and shipment.

  • Sakura Mint Trading does not purchase, reserve, or secure products or vehicles before quotation approval and payment confirmation.
  • Availability, supplier prices, shipping costs, exchange rates, and related fees may change before payment confirmation.
  • The buyer is responsible for confirming import legality, customs requirements, permits, registration, certification, use, and resale conditions in the destination country.
  • Import duties, taxes, customs fees, registration fees, inspection fees, storage fees, and destination-country charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless clearly stated in the quotation.
  • Sakura Mint Trading may refuse requests involving prohibited, restricted, unsafe, counterfeit, illegal, sanctioned, or unsuitable items.
  • Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee customs clearance, import approval, registration, certification, legal use, or resale in the buyer’s country.
How the Process Works

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Submit a sourcing or export support request

Provide your request details through the contact form or request form.

Step 2: Sakura Mint Trading reviews the request details

We review the submitted information and assess feasibility.

Step 3: A quotation is prepared

We prepare a quotation based on the requested items and required support.

Step 4: The buyer reviews and approves the quotation

Quotation approval is required before purchase or sourcing activities proceed.

Step 5: Payment instructions are provided

Payment instructions are shared after quotation review and approval.

Step 6: Purchase or sourcing activities begin after payment confirmation

All purchase or sourcing activities begin only after payment confirmation.

Step 7: Export-related coordination and shipping support proceeds as applicable

Export-related coordination and shipping assistance proceed based on the agreed scope.
