Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about Sakura Mint Trading’s Japan sourcing, quotation, payment, used vehicle sourcing, and export-related support services.

What does Sakura Mint Trading do?

Sakura Mint Trading helps overseas buyers request quotations for new Japanese products and used vehicles from Japan. We support sourcing requests, quotation preparation, supplier communication, purchase support, and export-related coordination after quotation approval and payment confirmation.

Can Sakura Mint Trading purchase items before payment?

Vehicle search and quotation preparation may take place before purchase authorization or payment. However, Sakura Mint Trading does not purchase new Japanese products or purchase, reserve, bid on, or otherwise commit to acquiring a used vehicle on the buyer’s behalf until the required quotation approval, payment confirmation, and any required final purchase authorization have been completed. We do not purchase a vehicle without the buyer’s authorization.

Do you support used vehicles from Japan?

Yes. Sakura Mint Trading supports used vehicle sourcing requests from Japan, subject to availability, supplier access, transaction conditions, and applicable laws.

Can you search for a used vehicle before I make payment?

Yes. We may search for and review available vehicle options and prepare a quotation before purchase authorization. However, we will not purchase, reserve, bid on, or otherwise commit to acquiring a vehicle on your behalf until the required quotation approval, payment confirmation, and any required final purchase authorization have been completed.

Why may I need to send bidding funds before a vehicle is found?

Some rare or limited-availability vehicles may appear in time-sensitive auctions. Because international payments may not arrive before the auction deadline, buyers who want Sakura Mint Trading to participate in vehicle auctions may be required to deposit sufficient bidding funds in advance.

Sakura Mint Trading will only bid within the maximum amount authorized by the buyer and within the confirmed available funds. Depositing funds does not guarantee a successful purchase. If a bid is unsuccessful, the remaining funds may be retained for a future purchase authorized by the buyer or refunded upon request, subject to applicable bank fees, intermediary bank fees, currency conversion differences, and other costs disclosed in advance.

Are vehicles from Japan right-hand drive?

Most vehicles sourced from Japan are right-hand drive (RHD). Buyers are responsible for confirming whether right-hand drive vehicles can be legally imported, registered, used, or resold in their destination country.

Are used vehicles guaranteed?

Used vehicles are supplied in their existing condition at the time of purchase. Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee that photos, auction sheets, supplier descriptions, mileage, repair history, or other information reveal every issue.

What vehicle information can I review before purchase?

Depending on what is available for the vehicle, you may review photos, mileage, vehicle specifications, condition information, auction sheet or inspection information when available, and supplier information. The amount and detail of information vary by vehicle and supplier, and the available information may not reveal every fault or issue.

Who is responsible for import duties and taxes?

The buyer is responsible for import duties, taxes, customs fees, registration fees, inspection fees, permits, certifications, storage fees, and other destination-country charges unless clearly stated in the quotation.

Can you guarantee customs clearance or registration in my country?

No. The buyer is responsible for confirming whether the requested product or vehicle can be legally imported, registered, used, or resold in the destination country. Sakura Mint Trading does not guarantee customs clearance, import approval, registration, certification, legal use, or resale.

Can you guarantee that a vehicle can be imported into my country?

No. Import requirements vary by destination and may change. Before authorizing a purchase, the buyer is responsible for confirming applicable vehicle age limits, right-hand drive restrictions, inspections, registration requirements, customs duties, taxes, certifications, and all other import conditions in the destination country.

Can prices change after a quotation?

Product and vehicle availability, supplier prices, shipping rates, exchange rates, taxes, fees, port-related costs, and other related costs may change before payment confirmation. If a material change affects the quoted transaction before a purchase or acquisition is committed, Sakura Mint Trading will inform the buyer and may issue a revised quotation for review and approval or cancel the quotation.

What products can I request?

Buyers may request new Japanese products, retail products, wholesale product requests, and used vehicles from Japan. Requests involving prohibited, restricted, unsafe, counterfeit, illegal, sanctioned, or unsuitable items may be refused.

How do I submit a request?

Buyers can submit a request through the Japan Sourcing Request Form on the Sakura Mint Trading website.

What information should I provide?

For product requests, please provide as much detail as possible, such as product name, brand, model number, reference URL, photos, desired quantity, budget, delivery country, and any important requirements. For a used vehicle request, please provide the make and model, preferred model year or range, budget, mileage preference, transmission, fuel type, destination country, destination port, and any other important requirements.

How does the process work?

For new Japanese products, the process is: Request → Quotation → Approval → Payment confirmation → Purchase → Export or shipping support. For used vehicles, the process is: Request → Vehicle search → Quotation → Buyer review → Approval → Payment confirmation → Final purchase authorization, if required → Purchase or acquisition → Export and shipping coordination. Vehicle search may take place before purchase authorization, but we do not commit to acquiring a vehicle until the required quotation approval, payment confirmation, and any required final purchase authorization have been completed.

Can I cancel after approval or payment?

Cancellation, changes, or refunds may not be possible after an authorized product purchase or a vehicle purchase, reservation, bid, or other acquisition commitment has been made or begun. If cancellation or refund is possible, costs already incurred may be deducted.

Do you handle shipping?

Sakura Mint Trading may support export-related coordination and shipping arrangements when applicable. Shipping conditions, costs, delivery times, and available methods depend on the item, destination country, carrier, and transaction conditions.
